Privacy Policy

This application is a personal, self-hosted tool used privately by its owner, running on the owner's own private infrastructure. It is not distributed, has no other users, and does not collect, share, or sell data from the public.

The application connects to a Google Sheets spreadsheet owned by the same person, using Google's standard OAuth authorization, strictly to read and write that individual's own spreadsheet. No data is shared with any third party.
