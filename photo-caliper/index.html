Photo Caliper

Measure real objects from a photo using a reference object of known size. No hardware needed.
How it works and accuracy tips
  1. Photograph the part straight on, phone parallel to the surface, with a reference of known size lying flat in the same plane as the feature you will measure.
  2. Press Set Reference, tap both ends of the reference, enter its real length.
  3. Use Distance for lengths, or Circle (three points on the edge) for hole and rim diameters.
  4. Press and drag on the photo to place a point. A magnifier shows the exact spot; release to drop it. Two fingers pinch to zoom and pan.
  5. Use Check Scale on a second known length. If the error is over 3%, the phone was tilted: retake the photo.

Depth is not measured. Take a separate photo for each plane, such as a top-down shot for hole sizes and a side shot with a ruler for height. Typical error is 1 to 3%.
